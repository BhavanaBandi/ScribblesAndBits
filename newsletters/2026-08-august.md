---
title: "Looking Back Without Wishing You Were Still There"
month: "August"

---

Dear Beautiful People,

There is something strange about entering a new season of life. Suddenly, the things you spent years worrying about are no longer quite as scary. The future that once felt so distant is slowly becoming the present and somehow, here I am, stepping into the corporate world, settling into a routine, and watching life become a little quieter.

And I think I'm finally learning that peace can feel unfamiliar when you've spent so long chasing the next thing.

For the longest time, I thought rest had to be earned. That I could slow down only after I had accomplished enough, worked hard enough, become enough. And now that life seems to have entered this strange, peaceful little era, a part of me still feels guilty for sitting still.

But maybe this is exactly when we are supposed to pause.

Not because there is nothing left to achieve — there is so much ahead of us. But because sometimes, before we climb the next mountain, we should turn around and look at the one we just climbed.

Look at the things that used to terrify us and no longer do.

The versions of ourselves we have quietly outgrown.

The friendships, heartbreaks, failures, victories and tiny moments that shaped us.

The things we once thought would break us, but somehow became stories we tell.

We are so obsessed with becoming that we rarely stop to acknowledge that we already have. Think about who you were a few years ago. Would they recognise the life you're living now? Would they believe the things you've survived, the person you've become, the things you've learned to let go of?

There is still a long way up. There are careers to build, cities to see, people to meet, mistakes to make, dreams to chase and versions of ourselves we haven't even met yet.

But perhaps growing doesn't always mean moving forward.

Sometimes, growing means looking back without wishing you were still there.

So, if life feels a little peaceful right now, don't rush to disturb it. You don't have to turn every quiet moment into another goal. You don't have to feel guilty for resting simply because there is still more to do.

There will always be more to climb.

For now, maybe we can just sit here for a moment, look at how far we've come, and let ourselves be proud.

And then, when we're ready, we can keep going.

Yours, with a smile,
Bhavana
