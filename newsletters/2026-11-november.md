Dear Beautiful People,

If I cannot keep you, I hope I can at least leave you gently.

Not because I have run out of reasons to stay, or because loving you has become difficult, or because I have suddenly found something better to do with all the love I have for you. Quite the opposite, actually. I think that is what makes this so difficult. I have never been more certain of how much I want something, and yet, I have never been more certain that I cannot have it.

There is a particular kind of heartbreak in knowing that two people can be right for each other and still be wrong for each other's lives.

We often talk about love as though it is the simplest thing in the world. Two people meet, they fall in love, and if they love each other enough, they find a way. We grow up believing that love conquers everything, that the only thing standing between two people is the courage to choose each other.

But nobody really tells you what happens when choosing each other means unchoosing everyone else.

What happens when the happiness of two people comes with the unhappiness of several others? When something that should be beautiful becomes something you have to explain, defend, and perhaps spend the rest of your life feeling guilty about? When you realise that the beginning of your happiness might look like the ending of someone else's?

I used to think that if I ever found something worth holding on to, I would hold on to it with both hands. I thought I would be the person who took the leap, who ignored the noise, who believed that life was too short to leave something beautiful unexplored.

But then you grow up, and you learn that other people's hearts are not collateral damage. That the people who have loved you, stood beside you, and made a home out of your ordinary days are not characters you can simply write out of a story because a new chapter has begun.

And perhaps the cruelest part is that I know what it feels like to be on the other side of this.

I know what it is like to watch something familiar become unfamiliar. To look at two people you trusted and realise that there is suddenly a world between you that you cannot enter. I know how quietly something like that can change the way you see people, how it can leave you questioning memories you once held very close. I know that some wounds don't announce themselves. They simply become a part of the person you grow into.

And I cannot bring myself to become the reason someone else has to learn that particular kind of sadness.

Maybe that is selfish of me. Maybe I am protecting a version of the past at the expense of a future that could have been wonderful. Maybe one day I will look back and wonder what would have happened if I had been a little braver.

I suspect I will wonder for a very long time.

Because you are not someone I want to forget. You are someone I wish I had met in a different story. One where there were no histories to consider, no familiar faces that would have to look away, no memories that would suddenly need new meanings. One where liking you could simply mean liking you. Where wanting to be with you did not require an explanation to anyone, including ourselves.

I wish I could have met you without everything that came before us.

I wish I could have loved you without having to measure the consequences.

I wish that wanting you was enough.

But it isn't.

And I think we have both known that for a while. There are conversations we have had without speaking, silences that have lasted a little longer than they should, and moments where I have had to remind myself that just because something feels like home does not mean I am allowed to move into it.

It is strange, isn't it? To have to exercise restraint with someone you would otherwise run towards.

To know exactly where you would like to be, and still make yourself walk in the opposite direction.

I don't know what to do with all the little things I will have to unlearn. The instinct to tell you something first. The things I see that I know would make you laugh. The conversations I will have in my head and never have out loud. The versions of ordinary days that I will occasionally imagine, even though I know better than to give them too much room.

I don't know how to turn a feeling into a memory when it hasn't even had the chance to become a life.

And I am sorry for that.

I am sorry that something so gentle has to end without ever really beginning. I am sorry that the only way I know to protect what we already have is to give up on what we could have had. I am sorry that I cannot promise you a different ending.

But please understand that letting you go does not mean that I wanted you any less.

If anything, I think it means I wanted you enough to understand that love cannot always be measured by how tightly we hold on. Sometimes it is measured by the things we are willing to give up, the people we refuse to hurt, and the futures we quietly bury without asking anyone to attend the funeral.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope you find someone who can choose you without hesitation, without complicated histories, without having to look over their shoulder to make sure nobody else is hurting. Someone who gets to love you out loud, in all the ordinary and extraordinary ways you deserve.

And I hope, somewhere along the way, you understand that this was never about you not being enough.

You were never the problem.

Perhaps in another life, we meet as strangers. Perhaps there, we get to have all the firsts we couldn't have here. Perhaps we get to be selfish, just once. Perhaps we get to choose each other without having to lose anything else.

Until then, I will keep the little pieces of this to myself. I will let the world carry on as it always has. I will laugh at the same jokes, show up to the same places, and pretend that some things don't make me think of you.

And maybe, eventually, they won't hurt quite as much.

But I don't think I will ever regret knowing you. Even if all we ever become is a story that neither of us gets to tell properly.

There is one thing I know I will never be able to look at quite the same way again.

Every time I see yellow and white, I will remember how u decided to choose me over everything else.

Yours, with a smile,

Bhavana
